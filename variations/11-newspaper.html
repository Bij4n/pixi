Images Now Tell Their Owners Where They’ve Been

New service embeds invisible tracking and cryptographic signatures into any photograph
Pixi, a new image tracking service, allows photographers, designers, and businesses to know exactly who opens and shares their images. The service embeds an invisible tracking pixel into any uploaded image and signs it using the C2PA content credential standard — the same technology trusted by Adobe, the BBC, and the New York Times. Every open, forward, and share is logged in real time, giving image owners unprecedented visibility into how their work travels across the internet. The service is free to start and requires no credit card.

How It Works

I. Upload

Any image. Tracking and C2PA signature added invisibly.

II. Share

Send anywhere. The image looks and works as normal.

III. Track

See every open and forward. Provenance is verifiable.
